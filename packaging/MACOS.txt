TPMExtractor fork on macOS

tpmextractor is a universal command-line program for Apple silicon and Intel Macs (macOS 11 or
later on Apple silicon, 10.15 or later on Intel). It is not signed with a developer certificate
or notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time you run a downloaded copy. Either:

- in Terminal, in the folder you unzipped, remove the download mark:

      xattr -d com.apple.quarantine tpmextractor

- or, in Finder, right-click (Control-click) tpmextractor, choose Open, then Open again
  (macOS 14 and earlier). On macOS 15 and later, run it once, then click "Open Anyway" in
  System Settings > Privacy & Security.

Then run it from Terminal, for example:

    ./tpmextractor --help
    ./tpmextractor /Volumes/TPM ~/tpm-dump

No game data is included: you need your own copy of the game. See README.md.
